# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing technical humility in interview-prep discussions

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sharing copyrighted book text verbatim
- Posting live interview questions under NDA or confidential company prompts
- Sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention of any kind

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and
will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code applies within all community spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers via GitHub Issues (use a private security
advisory if sensitive) or by contacting the repository owners listed in the
README. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction** — private warning and request to amend behavior
2. **Warning** — public or private warning; temporary interaction limits
3. **Temporary ban** — ban from community spaces for a specified period
4. **Permanent ban** — permanent ban for repeated or severe violations
